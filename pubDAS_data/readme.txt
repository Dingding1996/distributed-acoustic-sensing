## Valencia-IslaLink DAS datasetThis dataset contains the Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) data collected by the University of Michigan in collaboration with the Spanish National Geographic Institute and IslaLink S.A.## Data description: The Valencia-Islalink experiment used a pre-installed telecommunication fiber-optic cable operated by IslaLink Holding Iberia S.L. and connecting the Spanish peninsula to Mallorca Island from Valencia to Palma de Mallorca. From September 1st to September 15th, 2020, a Febus A1-R interrogator unit was connected to the Valencia side to sample the first 50 km of the cable. The cable location provided by the cable operator, indicates that the first 9,189 m are on land. This is easily confirmed with the observation of characteristic traffic noise in the record sections. According to the installation report, the remaining 40,811 m are buried about 1 m below the Mediterranean seabed. Data were acquired at a sampling frequency of 1000 Hz, with a gauge length of 30.4 m and a spatial resolution of 16.8 m, resulting in a dense seismic array of 2977 channels. The data from September 1st to September 7th are continuous and available on PubDAS. The remaining week was less complete with the presence of numerous recording gaps and was therefore not published.  ## Downsampling: The sampling rate of the original recording is 1000 Hz, and the duration of each file is 1 hour. In order to improve computational efficiency, we down-sampled the data to 250 Hz, and truncated an hour-long file into six 10-minute files. Before downsampling, in order to avoid aliasing, we first low-pass filter the data with a 10th-order Chebyshev type I low-pass filter (below 125 Hz). The filename of each file is the start time of the data.## Data format and reading:

The format of the data is Hierarchical Data Format version 5 (HDF5). We provide a Matlab example and code for reading data files (see Matlab_read_data_example).## Cable location:The location of the data is on the coast of Valencia in the Mediterranean Sea. Please refer to the two files “DAS-1-geometry-Valencia-undersea.csv” and “DAS-1-geometry-Valencia-onland.kmz” for specific latitude and longitude coordinates.## Open data license:The Valencia-Islalink DAS dataset is made available under the Open Database License: http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/.
